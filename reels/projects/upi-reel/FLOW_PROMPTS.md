## 0. Rules for 4-second shots

1. **Duration:** if Flow lets you pick the length, choose **4s**. If it only makes 8s, generate it and **keep the best 4 seconds**. The action is at the start of every prompt.
2. **One action per shot, max ~8 words of dialogue.** If Veo's Hindi sounds off, keep the shot and dub the line in CapCut.
3. **No text or logos inside the AI video.** Numbers, "PhonePe" and meme captions are CapCut overlays.
4. **Meme sounds** ("are baap re", "moye moye", phonk, sad trombone) come from **CapCut's or Instagram's audio library**, never ripped from movies or YouTube, or the reel can be muted.
5. **No real people.** These are meme *formats* acted by our own characters.
6. Test on **Veo 3.1 Fast**, regenerate keepers on **Quality**, and set **Portrait 9:16** every time.

## 2. Transitions

- **🔗 CHAIN:** the same scene continues. Use **Frames to Video** with **first frame = last frame of the previous shot**, so the cut is invisible.
- **⚡ FLASH:** the previous shot ends on full-frame light, and this shot starts on it. Cut on the brightest frame and add a 2–4 frame cross-dissolve in CapCut.
- **✂️ HARD CUT:** a deliberate meme cut. Don't smooth it.
- **🔁 Loop:** after shot 16 (the wink), the reel restarts on Bunty strutting in again and bragging again. It reads as "Bunty never learns", and every replay adds to your watch time.

Every prompt ends with the same style line. Keep it, because it keeps all 16 shots matching.

## 4. CapCut finishing

- **Trim each shot to 3.5–4s** and cut every dead frame. Trim Shot 1 to about 3s so the brag lands sooner.
- **Text:** huge bold font, max 5–6 words, inside the middle 70% of the frame. Key numbers in yellow.
- **Auto-captions** for every dialogue line.
- **Meme sounds:** phonk (shots 1, 15, 16), "Are baap re" (4), "Moye moye" (9), sad trombone (10), cosmic power-up (12). Keep them loud; they're the punchlines.
- Keep a small **`Secret 1/3 · 2/3 · 3/3`** tag in the top corner during shots 6–11.

## 5. Fact sheet (for the pinned comment and replies)

- PhonePe revenue from operations **₹7,114.86 crore in FY25** (PhonePe DRHP). This is *revenue*, not profit. PhonePe reported a net loss of about ₹1,172 crore in FY25, with positive adjusted profit. Say "revenue", never "profit".
- UPI hit a record **24.51 billion transactions in August 2026** (NPCI).
- PhonePe about **46%** and Google Pay about **33%** of UPI volume in May 2026, together **about 79–80%** (NPCI data).
- Soundbox rental is a real business for both Paytm and PhonePe: merchants pay a monthly subscription for the device.
- There's **zero MDR** (no merchant fee) on most UPI payments. PhonePe's money comes from merchant services (incl. smart speaker rental), lending and insurance distribution, platform fees on recharges and bills, and ads. It also gets small government UPI incentives (₹164.73 Cr in FY25).

Sources: [Finshots – PhonePe DRHP](https://finshots.in/archive/breaking-down-the-phonepe-drhp/) · [Inc42 – PhonePe profit puzzle](https://inc42.com/features/phonepe-profit-puzzle-upi-dominance-diversification/) · [IndMoney – DRHP explained](https://www.indmoney.com/blog/ipo/phonepe-drhp-explained) · [Free Press Journal – Aug 2026 UPI record](https://www.freepressjournal.in/india/upi-transactions-hit-record-2451-billion-in-august-festive-demand-drives-growth) · [Inc42 – May 2026 UPI share](https://inc42.com/buzz/upi-transactions-jump-3-8-mom-in-may-cross-23-bn-mark/) · [Outlook Business – share below 80%](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/economy-and-policy/phonepe-google-pay-combined-upi-market-share-drops-below-80-for-first-time)
