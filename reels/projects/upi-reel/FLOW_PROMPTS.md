# UPI Reel: "Speaker Babu" 🔊 (comedy + explainer)

**Length:** 50 seconds · **Format:** 9:16 vertical · **Language:** Hinglish
**Tool:** Google Flow (Veo 3.1) for the clips. Text overlays and music go on in CapCut.

## The idea
A funny mini-story at a chai stall, not a lecture.
**Bunty** pays ₹10 for chai by UPI and brags: *"UPI free hai, PhonePe toh charity karta hai!"*
The payment speaker on the counter, **Speaker Babu**, wakes up and roasts him with the facts: how the app actually earns.
It ends on a punchline every Indian has lived through (*"Chhutta nahi hai… UPI kar do"*) and loops back to the start.

**Why it's entertaining:** each character has a clear role (show-off, tired chaiwala, sassy robot speaker). It uses moments everyone recognises: the speaker shouting "₹10 prapt hue", "Better luck next time" scratch cards, "chhutta nahi hai". There's a joke every 7 seconds, and the facts arrive as roasts, so it never feels like a class.

---

## 0. Rules before you start

1. **Veo makes ~8-second clips.** This reel is 7 clips, about 7 seconds each (the last one is 8s), for 50s total. Generate each clip at 8s and trim it.
2. **No text or logos inside the AI video.** Veo garbles letters, and real brand logos are a trademark risk. The numbers, "PhonePe" and "Better luck next time" all go in as **CapCut text overlays**.
3. **Dialogue:** the lines are written inside the prompts so Veo animates the mouths and speaks them. Test on **Veo 3.1 – Fast** first. If Veo's Hindi sounds wrong, keep the clip and **dub the line yourself in CapCut**. The cartoon style hides small lip mismatches.
4. **Iterate on Fast, then regenerate the keeper once on Quality.**
5. Set aspect ratio to **Portrait 9:16** before every generation.

## 2. Making the transitions seamless

- **Shots 1→2, 5→6, 6→7:** use **Extend** on the previous clip (or **Frames to Video** with its last frame as the first frame). The scene keeps moving with no cut.
- **Shot 2→3 and 3→4:** "zoom into Speaker Babu's glowing eyes, purple flash, new world" and back. Cut on the brightest purple frame.
- **Shot 4→5:** whip pan to Bunty's phone. Cut on the blurriest frame of both clips.
- **Shot 7:** set **first frame = last frame of Shot 6** and **end frame = first frame of Shot 1**. The reel then **loops**: Bunty scans the QR again and the speaker chimes again. Every replay adds to your watch time.
- In CapCut, add a **2–4 frame cross-dissolve** on each cut to hide colour jumps.

## 4. CapCut finishing

- **Text:** huge bold font, maximum 5 words per overlay, inside the middle 70% of the frame (Instagram's buttons cover the bottom and right). Key numbers in yellow.
- **Captions:** add auto-captions for every dialogue line (many people watch on mute).
- **Music:** a light, playful comedy track from the Instagram or CapCut library at about 15% volume, ducked under dialogue. Keep Veo's sound effects loud. The spit-take, the sad trombone and the "chhutta" sting are the laughs.
- **Pacing:** cut every dead half-second. If a clip has a slow start, trim it.

## 5. Fact sheet (for the pinned comment and replies)

- PhonePe revenue from operations **₹7,114.86 crore in FY25** (PhonePe DRHP). This is *revenue*, not profit. PhonePe reported a net loss of about ₹1,172 crore in FY25, with positive adjusted profit. Say "revenue", never "profit".
- UPI hit a record **24.51 billion transactions in August 2026** (NPCI).
- PhonePe about **46%** and Google Pay about **33%** of UPI volume in May 2026, together **about 79–80%** (NPCI data).
- There's **zero MDR** (no merchant fee) on most UPI payments. PhonePe's money comes from merchant services (incl. smart speaker rental), lending and insurance distribution, platform fees on recharges and bills, and ads. It also gets small government UPI incentives (₹164.73 Cr in FY25).

Sources: [Finshots – PhonePe DRHP](https://finshots.in/archive/breaking-down-the-phonepe-drhp/) · [Inc42 – PhonePe profit puzzle](https://inc42.com/features/phonepe-profit-puzzle-upi-dominance-diversification/) · [IndMoney – DRHP explained](https://www.indmoney.com/blog/ipo/phonepe-drhp-explained) · [Free Press Journal – Aug 2026 UPI record](https://www.freepressjournal.in/india/upi-transactions-hit-record-2451-billion-in-august-festive-demand-drives-growth) · [Inc42 – May 2026 UPI share](https://inc42.com/buzz/upi-transactions-jump-3-8-mom-in-may-cross-23-bn-mark/) · [Outlook Business – share below 80%](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/economy-and-policy/phonepe-google-pay-combined-upi-market-share-drops-below-80-for-first-time)
