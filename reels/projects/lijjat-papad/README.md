# Lijjat Papad: "₹80 se ₹1,600 crore"

37.5 s colourful flat-illustration reel (1080×1920, 30 fps) for @mindset_mode_in. It has 14 hand-drawn SVG scenes, zoom, circle-wipe and whip transitions, kinetic text, Hinglish subtitles and synthesized SFX. No photos or AI images are used.

```bash
node tools/render.mjs projects/lijjat-papad --preview   # contact sheet
node tools/render.mjs projects/lijjat-papad             # -> output/lijjat-papad.mp4
```

Edit scenes in the `scenes` section of `index.html` (one `S.push({d, f(lt)})` per shot). Transition types are set in `TT`, subtitles in `SUBS`, and sound in the `cue(...)` list.

## Facts used
- Founded 15 March 1959 on a Mumbai building terrace by 7 Gujarati women, including Jaswantiben Jamnadas Popat.
- They borrowed ₹80 from social worker Chhaganlal Karamsi Parekh and made 4 packets on day one.
- About 100–150 members by year 2 and 300+ by year 3.
- Today about 45,000 members and a reported turnover of about ₹1,600 crore. These figures come from secondary sources (news and blogs), not audited filings, so say "reported" in the caption.

Sources: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shri_Mahila_Griha_Udyog_Lijjat_Papad), [The Better India](https://www.thebetterindia.com/274700/homemakers-start-iconic-lijjat-papad-brand-earn-crores/), [Impact on Net](https://www.impactonnet.com/impact-stories/roots-to-legacy-six-decades-of-lijjat-profit-and-female-ownership-16370.html)
