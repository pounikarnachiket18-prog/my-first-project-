How a day runs

Three day types

Built for about 9 study hours. Each day in the timetable is one of these. Mains mocks sit in the 9 to 12 slot because that is when most real shifts run.

Prelims mock day

  1. 06:30–07:00Warm-up: 15 simplification Qs + 1 puzzle
  2. 07:00–08:00Full mock, sectional timers on, phone in another room
  3. 08:20–10:20Analysis: re-solve every wrong and skipped Q without a timer, log each one
  4. 10:30–12:30Drill the weakest topic from today's analysis
  5. 14:00–15:00Current affairs + banking awareness
  6. 15:00–17:30Second mock or sectionals listed for the day, then its analysis
  7. 20:30–21:00Error log revision + 20 new words

Mains mock day

  1. 06:30–07:30Light revision only, nothing new
  2. 09:00–12:00Full mains mock (+30 min descriptive on PO days)
  3. 12:00–13:00Lunch away from screens
  4. 13:00–16:00Section-by-section analysis. Every missed GA fact goes into the GA notebook
  5. 16:30–18:00Two sectionals on the weakest mains section
  6. 20:00–21:00Current affairs + error log

Build or repair day

  1. 07:00–09:00Concept learning for the topics listed
  2. 09:15–12:30Sectionals at real timers, analysed after each one
  3. 14:00–16:00Re-solve the error log from the week
  4. 16:00–17:00Current affairs + static GK
  5. 17:30–19:00Topic drill or descriptive writing
  6. 20:30–21:00Plan tomorrow from the error log

Analysis rules

  • Analysis time is at least the mock time: 1 hr mock, 2 hr analysis; 3 hr mains, 3 hr analysis.
  • Tag every wrong or skipped Q: A concept gap, B knew it but too slow, C silly mistake, D should have skipped.
  • If prelims accuracy is below 85%, attempt fewer questions next mock, not more. Negative marking is 0.25 per wrong answer.
  • Track per mock: score, attempts, accuracy %, minutes per section, and your percentile if the series shows one.

If you only have 4 hours a day

  • Do only the first mock listed on each day and its analysis. Skip the second mock and the drills.
  • Never skip a mains mock or the level review on Sundays.
  • Move current affairs to your commute: 30 minutes of a daily CA quiz or capsule.
  • Use one test series per level so difficulty stays comparable from mock to mock.
